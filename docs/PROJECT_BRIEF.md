# Project brief

## User-facing outcome

A user finishes a call, receives a normalized meeting record, reviews separate CRM and Obsidian proposals, and deliberately delivers the useful parts to each system without copying everything everywhere.

## Primary user

Solo operators, consultants, founders and relationship-led teams using a meeting recorder alongside one or more CRMs and personal knowledge systems.

## Product thesis

The useful product is not another bot in the meeting. It is a small, opinionated information router after the meeting: recorder-neutral, destination-neutral, evidence-preserving and safe enough to trust with relationship context.

## Success criteria

- One real meeting can reach a quarantined Obsidian inbox in under five minutes of configuration.
- Replayed webhook events do not create duplicate notes or proposals.
- A user can see exactly what will be written, where and why.
- No raw transcript reaches the CRM by default.
- A second recorder or CRM can be added as an adapter without changing the canonical model.
